TV Remote

Control your Android TV from your phone.

DOWNLOAD APK

If the button does nothing, type this address into the Downloader app:

github.com/jaswanthkumarj1234-beep/tv-remote/releases/latest/download/tv-remote.apk

Install on the TV

  1. Easiest: install the free Downloader app from the TV's app store, open it, type the address above and press Go.
  2. When asked, allow Install unknown apps for Downloader (or your browser), then press Install.
  3. Open TV Remote. The TV shows an address like http://192.168.1.20:8080 and a 6-digit code.
  4. Press the orange button in the app to open Accessibility, then turn TV Remote on.

Then on your phone

  1. Join the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  2. Open the address shown on the TV in your phone's browser.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code and tap Save.
This app can type and press buttons on your TV, so only install it on TVs you own and pair only with phones you trust. It only works on your home network.
