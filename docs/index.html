TV Remote

Control your Android TV from your phone's browser: touchpad, text typing, volume, and voice commands.

Download APK for Android TV

Install on the TV

  1. On the TV, allow installs from unknown sources for your file manager or browser (Settings > Apps > Special access).
  2. Download tv-remote.apk on the TV's browser, or copy it from your phone with a USB stick.
  3. Open the file and install it.
  4. Open TV Remote on the TV. It shows an address like http://192.168.1.20:8080 and a 6-digit code.
  5. Open Settings > Accessibility > TV Remote and turn it on. The app's own button also opens this screen.

Use it from your phone

  1. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  2. Open the address shown on the TV in your phone's browser.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code, then tap Save.
  4. Use the touchpad, buttons, text box, or mic.
Note: This app can type into your TV and press buttons for you, so only install it on TVs you own and only pair with phones you trust. The phone controller is served by the TV itself, so it only works on your home network.

Known limits

  • Typing works in apps with a focused text field.
  • Arrow keys are approximated with swipes, not true remote key presses.
  • Play and pause aren't supported. Volume is.
  • Voice in the browser may need the phone keyboard's mic instead.
